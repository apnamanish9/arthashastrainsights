Service 01

Equity Research Reports

In-depth fundamental analysis of listed equities on NSE and BSE covering all market capitalisations.

Each equity research report covers: business model and competitive positioning, financial analysis (revenue, margins, return ratios), balance sheet strength, management quality assessment, industry dynamics, key risk factors, and a fair value estimate based on multiple valuation methodologies.

  • Business and industry overview
  • 5-year financial trend analysis
  • Valuation: DCF, P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B
  • Bear, base, and bull case scenarios
  • Key risks to the investment thesis
  • Full analyst disclosures and conflict-of-interest statement
⚠ Report Limitation

Equity research reports are not personalised investment recommendations. They reflect the analyst's opinion at the time of writing and are subject to change without notice. Fair value estimates involve assumptions that may prove incorrect. Investors must conduct their own due diligence.

Service 02

Technical Analysis

Chart-based analysis covering price action, key levels, and momentum for educational and informational purposes.

Technical analysis studies historical price and volume data to identify trends, patterns, and potential inflection points. Our technical notes cover key indices, sectors, and individual stocks. These are analytical observations, not trading signals or buy/sell recommendations.

  • Price action and trend analysis
  • Support and resistance identification
  • Volume analysis
  • Momentum indicators (RSI, MACD, etc.)
  • Chart pattern identification
  • Potential entry/exit zones (educational only)
⚠ Technical Analysis Limitation

Technical analysis does not guarantee future price movements. Past chart patterns do not predict future outcomes. Technical notes are for informational and educational use only — not trading advice. All trading decisions are the sole responsibility of the investor.

Service 03

Sector Analysis

Macro-level sector research covering industry dynamics, competitive landscape, and valuation benchmarks across 15+ sectors.

Understanding sector dynamics is as important as analysing individual stocks. Our sector reports provide a comprehensive view of the competitive environment, regulatory framework, demand-supply dynamics, and relative valuations within each sector.

  • Sector outlook and growth drivers
  • Regulatory and policy environment
  • Key players and competitive positioning
  • Sector valuation benchmarks
  • Risks: cyclical, regulatory, macro
  • Comparative analysis of listed companies in the sector
