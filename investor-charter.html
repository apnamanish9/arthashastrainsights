A Services Provided by Arthashastra Insights

B Rights of Investors

As a subscriber or user of Arthashastra Insights' research services, you are entitled to the following rights:

B.1 Right to Transparent Disclosures

  • The right to know the SEBI registration number of the Research Analyst (INH000024620).
  • The right to receive full disclosure of any conflicts of interest the analyst or the firm has in securities covered in research reports.
  • The right to know whether the analyst holds any position in the securities covered.
  • The right to understand the methodology and assumptions underlying any research report.

B.2 Right to Fair Treatment

  • The right to receive research reports that are based on honest, independent, and objective analysis.
  • The right not to be subjected to misleading, exaggerated, or unsubstantiated claims.
  • The right to receive research that clearly discloses it is not personalised investment advice.
  • The right to be informed of changes to research views or fair value estimates in a timely manner.

B.3 Right to Grievance Redressal

  • The right to lodge a complaint with Arthashastra Insights and receive a response within 30 calendar days.
  • The right to escalate unresolved complaints to SEBI SCORES (scores.sebi.gov.in).
  • The right to seek dispute resolution through the Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) mechanism at smartodr.in.

B.4 Right to Data Privacy

  • The right to have personal information handled confidentially and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
  • The right to request access to, or deletion of, personal data by contacting apnamanish9@gmail.com.

As a SEBI Registered Research Analyst, Arthashastra Insights commits to the following obligations:

  • Maintain SEBI registration and comply with all applicable regulations, circulars, and guidelines issued by SEBI from time to time.
  • Disclose conflicts of interest in every research report as required under SEBI (Research Analyst) Regulations, 2014, Regulation 19.
  • Ensure research reports are based on independent analysis and are not influenced by the subject company or any third party.
  • Display the SEBI registration number (INH000024620) prominently on all communications, research reports, and this website.
  • Clearly state in every report that it is for informational purposes only and not personalised investment advice.
  • Clearly state in every report the basis and assumptions of the research view.
  • Not promise, assure, or imply guaranteed returns, profits, or specific investment outcomes.
  • Not use language such as "guaranteed returns," "risk-free," "sure-shot," or similar phrases in any research or marketing communication.
  • Redress investor complaints within 30 calendar days of receipt.
  • Publish and update investor charter, disclaimer, grievance redressal mechanism, and investor charter on the website.
  • Submit required reports and disclosures to SEBI on time.

D Do's for Investors

  • ✓ Always verify the SEBI registration of any Research Analyst before subscribing to their services. You can verify at sebi.gov.in.
  • ✓ Read research reports carefully, including all disclosures, assumptions, risk factors, and the disclaimer section.
  • ✓ Use research as one input among many. Conduct your own independent due diligence before investing.
  • ✓ Consider your own risk tolerance, investment horizon, and financial goals before making any investment decision.
  • ✓ Consult a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser (RIA) for personalised investment advice tailored to your financial situation.
  • ✓ Keep records of research subscriptions, payments made, and reports received.
  • ✓ Register complaints on SEBI SCORES (scores.sebi.gov.in) if not satisfied with the RA's response.

E Don'ts for Investors

  • ✗ Do not invest solely on the basis of a research report without independent verification. Research is one input, not a directive.
  • ✗ Do not assume that a SEBI registration number guarantees the accuracy of research or the profitability of any investment.
  • ✗ Do not invest money you cannot afford to lose. Equity investments are high risk and illiquid in adverse conditions.
  • ✗ Do not believe claims of "guaranteed returns," "risk-free returns," "sure-shot tips," or any similar language — these are red flags and violations of SEBI regulations.
  • ✗ Do not share personal financial information or make payments to unregistered entities claiming to be research analysts or advisers.
  • ✗ Do not rely on past performance of research reports as an indicator of future results.
  • ✗ Do not confuse a Research Analyst with an Investment Adviser. An RA publishes research; an RIA provides personalised advice.

F Investor Awareness

Arthashastra Insights is committed to investor education and awareness as part of its obligations under SEBI guidelines. The following resources are available:

F.1 Key Definitions

  • Research Analyst (RA): A SEBI registered entity that prepares and publishes research reports on securities for informational purposes. Cannot provide personalised investment advice.
  • Investment Adviser (RIA): A SEBI registered entity that provides personalised investment advice based on a client's financial profile. Requires Know Your Client (KYC) documentation.
  • Portfolio Manager (PMS): A SEBI registered entity that manages the investment portfolio of clients on a discretionary or non-discretionary basis.
  • SEBI SCORES: Centralised online system for investor grievances — available at scores.sebi.gov.in.

F.2 Red Flags to Watch Out For

⚠ Warning Signs of Fraudulent Schemes

Be alert to the following red flags when dealing with any entity claiming to be a Research Analyst:

  • Promises of guaranteed returns or "no-loss" strategies — this is illegal and a violation of SEBI regulations.
  • Requests to transfer funds to personal bank accounts in exchange for "tips."
  • Claims of having insider information about upcoming results or corporate events.
  • Pressure to act immediately or "miss out" on a "once-in-a-lifetime" opportunity.
  • Inability to provide SEBI registration details when asked.
  • Operating only on WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, or social media without a proper website and SEBI registration.

If you encounter such behaviour from any entity, report it to SEBI immediately at scores.sebi.gov.in or call SEBI's investor helpline at 1800 266 7575.
