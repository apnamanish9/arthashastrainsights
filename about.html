Our Story

Rooted in Research, Guided by Integrity

Arthashastra Insights was founded with a clear purpose: to democratise access to independent, professional-grade equity research for Indian investors.

The name "Arthashastra" draws from Chanakya's classical treatise on governance, economics, and statecraft — a body of work that emphasised objective analysis, disciplined strategy, and long-term thinking. We carry these values into modern financial research.

In a market landscape often clouded by noise, bias, and unverified claims, our mission is to provide research that is transparent, methodology-driven, and fully compliant with SEBI's Research Analyst Regulations, 2014.

"Arthashastra Insights was built on one principle: every research report must be something we would be comfortable publishing under regulatory scrutiny and that we would hand to a family member making an investment decision."

— Founder, Arthashastra Insights
